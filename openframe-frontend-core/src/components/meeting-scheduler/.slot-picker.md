Day layer (the lib's ODS `Calendar` — react-day-picker wrapper) + a day heading and a scrollable time-chip grid of `Button`s. Month navigation drives the host-proxy `monthOffset` (0..`MAX_MONTH_OFFSET`); days without slots are disabled. Exports `dayKeyInZone(ms, timeZone)` — the parent uses it to auto-select the first available day. All instants are epoch-ms; labels render in the parent-resolved zone (this component never reads Intl zone itself). See [`docs/EMBEDDING_HUBSPOT_MEETINGS.md`](../../../docs/EMBEDDING_HUBSPOT_MEETINGS.md).
